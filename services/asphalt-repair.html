Expert Asphalt Repair Services in Kansas City Metro

Potholes and damaged asphalt create safety hazards and project a poor image to customers. Precision Care provides fast, professional asphalt repair services throughout Kansas City to restore your parking lot's safety and appearance.

Our Asphalt Repair Services

  • Emergency pothole patching
  • Asphalt overlay and resurfacing
  • Full-depth repairs for severe damage
  • Edge and curb repairs
  • Base repair and stabilization
  • Infrared asphalt repair
  • ADA-compliant ramp repairs

Common Asphalt Problems We Fix

  • Potholes: Dangerous depressions that damage vehicles
  • Alligator Cracking: Network of cracks indicating base failure
  • Rutting: Depressions in wheel paths from heavy traffic
  • Raveling: Surface disintegration and aggregate loss
  • Edge Deterioration: Crumbling edges and perimeter damage
  • Settlements: Sinking areas from poor base support

Our Repair Process

  1. Assessment: Thorough inspection of damage and underlying causes
  2. Preparation: Removal of damaged asphalt and base preparation
  3. Base Repair: Stabilization and compaction of sub-base if needed
  4. Hot Asphalt Application: Installation of high-quality hot mix asphalt
  5. Compaction: Professional rolling and compaction for durability
  6. Finishing: Edge blending and surface finishing
  7. Striping: Line repainting if needed

Why Immediate Repair is Important

  • Prevents further damage and costly repairs
  • Reduces liability from trip and fall accidents
  • Protects vehicles from damage
  • Maintains professional property appearance
  • Extends overall pavement life
