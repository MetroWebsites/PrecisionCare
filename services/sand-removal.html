Expert Sand Removal Services in Kansas City Metro

After harsh Kansas City winters, sand accumulation can make your parking lot look unprofessional and create safety hazards. Precision Care provides thorough post-winter sand removal and cleanup services throughout the KC metro area.

Our Sand Removal Process

  • Complete parking lot assessment and planning
  • Industrial-strength sweeping equipment
  • Edge and curb line cleaning
  • Storm drain clearing to prevent clogs
  • Multiple passes for thorough removal
  • Proper disposal of collected materials
  • Final inspection and touch-up

Why Post-Winter Cleanup Matters

Sand and debris left on your parking lot after winter can:

  • Create an unprofessional appearance
  • Damage asphalt surfaces over time
  • Clog drainage systems
  • Accelerate pavement deterioration
  • Create dust and air quality issues
