Expert Sand Removal Services

After harsh Kansas City winters, sand accumulation can make your parking lot look unprofessional and create safety hazards. Precision Care provides thorough post-winter sand removal and cleanup services throughout the KC metro area.

Winter sand and debris left on your parking lot damages your asphalt, clogs drainage systems, and creates an unprofessional appearance. Our industrial-strength sweeping equipment and experienced crew ensure complete removal of all winter residue, restoring your property to pristine condition.

Our Sand Removal Process:

  • Complete Assessment: Thorough inspection and planning
  • Industrial Equipment: High-powered sweeping machines
  • Edge Cleaning: Detailed curb line and edge cleanup
  • Drain Clearing: Storm drain cleaning to prevent clogs
  • Multiple Passes: Thorough removal with quality checks
  • Proper Disposal: Environmentally responsible disposal
  • Final Inspection: Quality control and touch-ups

Why Post-Winter Cleanup Matters

Sand and debris left on your parking lot after winter can:

  • Create an unprofessional appearance for customers
  • Accelerate asphalt deterioration and surface damage
  • Clog storm drains causing flooding issues
  • Create dust clouds affecting air quality
  • Damage landscaping and surrounding areas
  • Track into buildings creating cleaning problems
