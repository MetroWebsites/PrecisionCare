Expert Crack Filling Services in Kansas City Metro

Cracks in your parking lot allow water to penetrate the base, leading to expensive repairs. Precision Care provides professional crack filling and sealing services throughout Kansas City to protect your investment and extend pavement life.

Our Crack Filling Process

  • Thorough inspection and assessment
  • Crack cleaning and preparation
  • Hot rubberized crack sealant application
  • Professional routing for larger cracks
  • Edge sealing and finishing
  • Quality control inspection
  • Traffic management and safety protocols

Types of Cracks We Repair

  • Linear Cracks: Straight cracks from thermal expansion
  • Alligator Cracking: Interconnected cracks indicating base failure
  • Edge Cracks: Perimeter cracking from poor drainage
  • Block Cracking: Large rectangular cracks
  • Settlement Cracks: Cracks from base settlement

Benefits of Professional Crack Filling

  • Prevents water infiltration and base damage
  • Extends pavement life by 3-5 years
  • Prevents small cracks from becoming potholes
  • Reduces liability from trip hazards
  • Improves property appearance
  • Cost-effective maintenance solution
