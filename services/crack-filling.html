Expert Crack Filling Services

Cracks in your parking lot allow water to penetrate the base, leading to expensive repairs. Precision Care provides professional crack filling and sealing services throughout Kansas City to protect your investment and extend pavement life.

Even small cracks become big problems when water infiltrates the base layer. Our hot rubberized crack sealant creates a flexible, waterproof barrier that prevents water damage, extends pavement life by 3-5 years, and stops small cracks from becoming costly potholes.

Our Crack Filling Process:

  • Thorough Inspection: Complete assessment of all cracks
  • Crack Cleaning: Remove debris and prepare surface
  • Hot Rubberized Sealant: Premium crack seal material
  • Professional Routing: Widen larger cracks for better seal
  • Edge Sealing: Complete coverage and finishing
  • Quality Control: Final inspection and touch-ups
  • Traffic Management: Safe work zones during service

Types of Cracks We Repair

  • Linear Cracks: Straight cracks from thermal expansion/contraction
  • Alligator Cracking: Interconnected cracks indicating base failure
  • Edge Cracks: Perimeter cracking from poor drainage
  • Block Cracking: Large rectangular crack patterns
  • Settlement Cracks: Cracks from base layer settlement

Benefits of Professional Crack Filling

  • Prevents water infiltration and base damage
  • Extends pavement life by 3-5 years
  • Stops small cracks from becoming potholes
  • Reduces liability from trip hazards
  • Improves property appearance
  • Most cost-effective maintenance solution
