Expert Seal Coating Services

Seal coating is the most cost-effective way to protect and extend the life of your asphalt parking lot. Precision Care provides professional seal coating services throughout Kansas City to keep your pavement looking great and lasting longer.

Like sunscreen protects your skin, seal coating protects your asphalt from UV rays, water damage, and chemicals. Our two-coat application process using premium sealers restores the rich black color while doubling your pavement's lifespan—the smartest investment you can make in your parking lot.

Our Seal Coating Process:

  • Complete Surface Cleaning: Thorough preparation and cleaning
  • Crack Filling: Repair all cracks before sealing
  • Edge Trimming: Remove grass and debris from edges
  • Premium Sealer Application: Coal tar or asphalt emulsion
  • Two-Coat Application: Maximum protection and longevity
  • Professional Edging: Clean lines and finishing
  • Traffic Control: Safe work zones and area protection
  • Fresh Striping: New lines after curing

Benefits of Seal Coating

  • UV Protection: Blocks harmful sun rays that oxidize asphalt
  • Water Resistance: Prevents water penetration and base damage
  • Chemical Resistance: Protects against oil, gas, and salt damage
  • Extended Life: Doubles the life of your parking lot
  • Improved Appearance: Restores rich, black color for curb appeal
  • Increased Flexibility: Reduces cracking from temperature changes
  • Easier Maintenance: Simplifies snow removal and cleaning
  • Cost Savings: Prevents expensive repairs and replacement

When Should You Seal Coat?

New Asphalt: Wait 6-12 months for proper curing before first seal coat

Existing Asphalt: Every 2-3 years for optimal protection

Best Season: Late spring through early fall when temperatures are above 50°F

Signs You Need Seal Coating:

  • Faded, gray appearance instead of rich black
  • Small surface cracks beginning to appear
  • Visible aggregate (rocks) showing through surface
  • Oil spots and chemical stains present
  • Rough, dry surface texture developing
