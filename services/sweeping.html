Professional Contract Sweeping Services

Precision Care provides reliable contract sweeping services for commercial properties throughout the Kansas City metro area. Regular sweeping keeps your parking lot clean, safe, and professional-looking year-round.

A clean parking lot creates a positive first impression for your customers and clients. Our professional sweeping services remove debris, trash, and dirt to maintain a pristine appearance that reflects well on your business.

Our Sweeping Services Include:

  • Scheduled Service - Daily, weekly, or monthly sweeping plans
  • Trash Removal - Complete litter and debris pickup
  • Debris Cleanup - Leaves, dirt, and loose materials
  • Curb Line Sweeping - Thorough edge cleaning
  • Storm Drain Cleaning - Prevent drainage issues
  • Seasonal Services - Adapted to Kansas City weather
  • Event Cleanup - Before and after special events
  • Emergency Sweeping - Available 24/7
